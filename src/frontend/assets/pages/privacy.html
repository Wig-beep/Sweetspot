Privacy

Draft. The organisation that provides SweetSpot must check this notice, add its details (for example, about its web server) and publish it.

Your data stays on your device

SweetSpot does not collect, store or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

  • Files you open (for example, a CSV file or an export from LibreView, Dexcom Clarity or Nightscout) are read by your browser on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, even though the button says "Upload a file".
  • Changes you try and their results stay on the page. They are gone when you close it.
  • Your device remembers one thing: that you hid the introduction, so that it is not shown again.
  • Using SweetSpot offline: your browser keeps the files of SweetSpot itself (not your data) so that it works without a connection.

Scenarios shared by educators

A scenario shared as a link holds the scenario in the part of the link after the "#". Browsers do not send this part to the website. Scenarios hold the texts the educator wrote and times of day, with no dates and no names.

The website that provides SweetSpot

Like any website, the web server that provides SweetSpot may keep standard records of requests, such as the time and your IP address. [The organisation that provides SweetSpot to add who runs the web server, what it records, and for how long.]
